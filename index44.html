From Chart to Insight

Data Is Not the Same as an Answer

A number on a slide is not an insight. An insight explains what matters and why — and points toward a decision.

Weak Statement

Sales decreased by 8%.

Analytical Insight

Sales decreased by 8%, primarily because repeat purchases declined among high-value customers in the northern region.

Data

Northern-region transaction records for the last two quarters, by customer and order value.

Information

Repeat-purchase rate among high-value northern customers fell from 61% to 44% quarter over quarter.

Insight

The regional revenue decline is concentrated in a specific, valuable segment losing loyalty — not a broad market slowdown.

Recommendation

Launch a targeted retention intervention for high-value northern accounts and monitor repeat-purchase rate weekly for 90 days.

Decision

Leadership commits budget and a named owner to the retention program, with a 90-day review checkpoint.

      Interactive Executive Case

      Apply the Framework

      Work through the case below, then reveal a suggested answer to compare your reasoning.

      Scenario: A company is experiencing falling customer retention, rising service costs, and uneven performance across regions. The Board has asked for an analysis before the next quarterly review.

      Suggested Answer — One Reasonable Path (Not the Only Correct One)

      Business QuestionWhich customer segments and regions are driving the retention decline, and is the rising service cost a cause or a consequence?
      TechniquesCohort analysis (retention by signup cohort), segmentation (value tier × region), variance analysis (service cost vs. budget by region), root-cause analysis on the worst-performing region.
      VisualizationsCohort retention heatmap, regional bar chart with target line, box plot of service cost per ticket by region to expose variation, Pareto chart of complaint categories.
      Executive InsightRetention is falling fastest among high-value customers in two regions where service costs have also risen — the data suggests a service-quality breakdown, not a pricing or product issue.
      RecommendationFund a service-recovery program in the two affected regions, reallocating budget from lower-priority initiatives; owner named at the regional VP level; 90-day checkpoint.
      KPI to MonitorHigh-value customer retention rate in the two affected regions, baseline vs. target, tracked monthly for two quarters.
